The Fusion Gap Report

Ten gaps stand between nuclear fusion and the electricity grid: net energy gain, plasma stability, materials, tritium breeding, magnets, heat-to-power conversion, private investment, regulation, supply chain and workforce. Each month I score every gap from 0 to 10, compare countries, and update an estimate of when the first fusion plant connects to the grid.

How the scores move. A score changes only on a new physical demonstration or an institutional decision with legal effect. Announcements, simulations, design papers and single funding rounds don't move scores. Each issue explains every score, lists its sources, and records corrections.

Scores are analyst judgement grounded in cited milestones, not an official index. Each issue works best on a laptop; on a phone, use the table view.
