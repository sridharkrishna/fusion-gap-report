# Geopolitics and Commercialisation of Nuclear Fusion: A Monthly Tracker

A monthly tracker of how far nuclear fusion is from the electricity grid.

Ten gaps stand in the way: net energy gain, plasma stability, materials science, tritium
breeding, magnet technology, heat-to-power conversion, private investment, regulatory
clarity, supply chain and workforce. Each issue scores every gap from 0 to 10 against a
2020 baseline, compares the US, Europe, China, Japan, South Korea and India, and updates an
estimate of when the first fusion plant connects to the grid.

**Live:** https://sridharkrishna.github.io/fusion-gap-report/

## How the scores move

A score changes only on a new physical demonstration or an institutional decision with
legal effect. Announcements, simulations, design papers and single funding rounds don't
move scores. Private Investment moves on capital with legal effect (a plant's financial
close, a binding power purchase agreement, an enacted public appropriation) or when the
Fusion Industry Association's annual cumulative total crosses into a new band. Each issue
explains every score, lists its sources, and keeps a log of corrections.

Scores are analyst judgement grounded in cited milestones, not an official index.

## Running it

Static HTML, no build step. Each issue page loads Chart.js 4.4.1 from cdnjs and Inter from
Google Fonts; everything else is inline.
